# AnalogAI

> "The sophistication of an agent, with the simplicity of a notebook."

AnalogAI is a fully local, agentic assistant designed to take control of your digital workflow 
without the friction of "productivity" apps. 

### Core Tenets:
- **Local First:** No foreign APIs, no subscriptions, no tracking.
- **Lightweight:** Runs on 6GB VRAM or CPU. Moves off the GPU when you need to work.
- **Zero Friction:** No learning curve. Plug and play.
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** Automated drafts for WhatsApp, Email, and Discord that wait for your approval.
